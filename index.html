What are GF Points?
GF Points are GRAMFY reward points. You earn them when a bot session ends — they are your share record for the future.
How do I earn GF Points?
When your session ends, you earn GF Points equal to your session's max loss, plus any profit the session made. Example: a $5 session earns 5 GF Points — and if it also made $5 profit, you earn 10 GF Points.
What will GF Points give me in the future?
GF Point holders will get future benefits: airdrop allocation, trading fee discounts and early access to new GRAMFY features. More points = a bigger share.
